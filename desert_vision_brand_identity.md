# 4 — THE LOGO

## 4.1 — Concept

The Desert Vision logo is **an eye that watches and is watched**.

The eye is the act of seeing — culturally, musically, emotionally. The star pupil is the moment of focus, the spark that makes a sound recognizable in a crowded room.

The current logo is a **refreshed version** of the original mark — same structure, cleaner execution, refined for scale and reproduction across digital and physical media.

## 4.2 — What the refresh changes

The visual identity went through a **light refresh** in May 2026.

Compared to the original:

- **Linework cleaned.** No more freehand irregularities — every curve is built on a geometric grid.
- **Stroke weights unified.** All elements of the symbol share the same weight (6px at base scale).
- **Star pupil rebuilt.** Reconstructed as a symmetric 4-point star on grid, replacing the previous irregular execution.
- **Typography modernized.** Geometric sans-serif with increased tracking for legibility at small sizes.
- **Balanced proportions.** Symbol-to-wordmark ratio recalibrated.

> 🇮🇹 *Il rebranding è stato leggero, non radicale. Il logo è riconoscibile come continuazione del precedente, ma più pulito, scalabile e professionale. La struttura — occhio + pupilla a stella + wordmark — resta intatta.*

## 4.3 — Logo components

The complete logo system consists of:

1. **Primary lockup** — eye symbol + "desert vision" wordmark stacked vertically
2. **Symbol only** — the eye, used for avatars, favicons, vinyl center labels, watermarks
3. **Wordmark only** — for textual contexts, footers, signatures

# 5 — VISUAL SYSTEM

## 5.1 — Colors

The brand operates on a strict two-color system:

- **Background dark:** `#0a0a0a` (warm black, not pure black)
- **Foreground light:** `#f2f0eb` (warm white, not pure white)

For release artwork, accent colors are introduced contextually — drawn from the world of the specific release (city, mood, artist).

## 5.2 — Typography

**Primary typeface:** Geometric sans-serif
Recommended: Futura, Avenir Next, Century Gothic, or IBM Plex Mono for technical contexts

**Wordmark rules:**
- Always lowercase: `desert vision`
- Tracking elevated (letter-spacing minimum 4–5px at display size)
- Never italic, never bold beyond medium
- Never decorative variants

## 5.3 — Voice and tone

Desert Vision communicates with:

- **Density over volume** — every word earns its place
- **Cryptic clarity** — clear meaning, indirect delivery
- **No didactic captions** — trust the audience to decode
- **No trend chasing** — the brand has held the same line for 6 years

Avoid:
- Hashtag soup
- Emoji-driven captions
- Festival/EDM marketing language
- Corporate music industry tone

# 6 — RELEASE ARTWORK SYSTEM

Each release follows a recognizable framework with controlled variation. Like a publication that keeps its grid but changes its cover.

## What stays fixed
- The Desert Vision logo (refresh version)
- Vertical and horizontal grid structure
- The wordmark in the lower portion of the frame
- 1:1 format for cover art

## What varies per release
- Background world (city, texture, environment)
- Iris content (the eye becomes a window into the release's universe)
- Accent palette (drawn from the release's identity)
- Symbolic objects within the composition

## Example — Stroopwafel (KITA Remix), May 2026
- Background: NYC subway lines, perpendicular grid only
- Iris: stroopwafel waffle pattern (caramel tones)
- Accent colors: official NYC MTA line colors
- Result: Desert Vision frame containing a Brooklyn world

> 🇮🇹 *Il sistema è come la homepage di Google: stessa struttura, mondi diversi. Ogni release è riconoscibile come Desert Vision a prima vista, ma ognuna ha la sua atmosfera.*

# 10 — VISUAL DON'TS

When designing for Desert Vision, never:

- Use generic EDM aesthetics
- Apply cyberpunk neon clichés
- Mimic festival marketing
- Add gradients to the logo
- Reproduce the logo with stroke weights below 1.5px at any scale
- Place the logo on a busy background without a clear protective frame
- Translate "desert vision" into other languages
- Use the wordmark in uppercase

---

# 11 — VISUAL DO'S

- Treat negative space as a design element
- Let one strong idea breathe rather than crowd the frame
- Honor the geometry of the grid
- Use real-world references (transit systems, food, architecture, signage)
- Combine at least two distant cultural worlds in every artwork
- Maintain warmth — Desert Vision is not cold minimalism, it is **deliberate minimalism with soul**
